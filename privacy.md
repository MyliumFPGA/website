## Who is responsible

Mylium operates mylium.eu and is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}).

## When you contact us

If you email us, we use the data in your message (such as your name, email address, organisation and project description) to answer your enquiry and to take steps towards a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). We keep it only as long as needed for that purpose or as required by law.

## Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google. It sets analytics cookies only after you select **Accept** (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Until then the Google tag runs in consent mode with storage denied; it may still send cookieless requests to Google, which include technical data such as your IP address and browser type. You can change your choice at any time with **Cookie settings** in the footer. Your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage.

## Third-party resources

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the engineering tools load libraries from public content delivery networks. These providers receive your IP address when your browser requests the files. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub processes server logs including IP addresses. Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area.

## Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction of processing and data portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent. You also have the right to complain to your data protection supervisory authority.
